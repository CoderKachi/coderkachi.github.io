Slime Shinobi is a simple isometric slash ‘em up, created for the sake of gaining hands-on experience Unreal Engine 5. The team consisted of 2 members where both of us were involved in all aspects of the game, whether this meant helping to design blueprints or actually implementing them. We wanted this to be a short project so we aimed to complete everything within 5 weeks.
