SLIME SHINOBI

Fruit ninja, but it’s a isometric dash and slash with slimes instead of fruits.

Blueprints
Unreal Engine 5
Game Development
2025

Slime Shinobi is a simple isometric slash ‘em up, created for the sake of gaining hands-on experience Unreal Engine 5. The team consisted of 2 members where both of us were involved in all aspects of the game, whether this meant helping to design blueprints or actually implementing them.

Showcase

Key Contributions

I created a blueprint system to save and load player data, such as their highest score. I also set up an isometric camera that follows the player and implemented the slashing sound effect for their primary attack.

To give players clear feedback during combat, I added visual indicators to enemies showing when and where they would attack. An outer ring displayed the enemy's attack range, while an inner ring signaled when the attack would be executed. [Video]

I implemented a wave system that spawns enemies at the start of each wave and scales their stats to progressively increase difficulty.

Enemy spawns were driven by a spawn credit system, where stronger enemies consumed more credits. This ensured early waves featured smaller enemies, while later waves introduced larger, tougher ones.

I primarily focused on the in-game HUD, building a set of reusable widgets to simplify UI development. The health, slow-motion, and dash meters all extend from a flexible base widget that supports features like fading or instantly hiding bars, customisable colors and an optional divider at a specified point.
