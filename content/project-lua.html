S T E L L A Prototype

Key Features

I implemented Procedural Level Generation mostly by using Binary Space Partitioning to divide and create rooms and a Minimum Spanning Tree algorithm to help ensure all rooms can be reached.

Objects could be easily placed onto the level since it was all grid / room based. I had a variation of rooms and tiles that would be placed as the final steps of the generation process.

Projectiles are "simulated" using raycasts. This makes them fairly performant and allows for a high level of control of their behaviour, such as predictable ricochet angles off walls.

I implemented some camera recoil to compliment the partical effects.

I incorporated some basic procedural animations to complement the static animations that the custom chacacters used. The head would be look towards the mouse and was clamped to not look un-natural when players attempted to look backwards while the entire character would tilted towards the direction they were rotating towards during motion.

I implemented some basic AI that would shoot at the player via the Weapon Framework. Naturally they can only do this when the player is within range and in line of sight. If the player falls out of their line of sight they move towards the last position they saw the player at.
