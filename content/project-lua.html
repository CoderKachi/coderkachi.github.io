ROBLOX Studio was one of the main entry points that got me into programming, mostly by modding existing scripts which eventually progressed to creating my own scripts and experimental projects from the ground up.

S T E L L A Prototype

Key Features

I used Binary Space Partitioning to divide the map into rooms and a Minimum Spanning Tree algorithm to ensure all rooms are connected and accessible.

The level’s grid and room-based structure made placing objects straightforward. A variety of pre-defined rooms and tile types were applied during the final stages of generation.

Projectiles are "simulated" using raycasts. This makes them fairly performant and allows for a high level of control of their behaviour, such as predictable ricochet angles off walls.

I implemented some camera recoil to compliment the partical effects.

Weapon Handling

I implemented simple AI that attacks the player using the Weapon Framework. Enemies shoot only when the player is within range and visible. If the player breaks line of sight, they move toward the last known position.

C O M P L E X Prototype

Key Features

I added simple procedural animations to enhance the static ones used by my custom characters. The head automatically tracks the mouse within a clamped range to avoid unnatural twisting, and the body subtly tilts in the direction of rotation while moving.

Procedural Animation while Walking

I implemented a system to hide rooms and characters outside the player’s line of sight. Additionally, walls that partially obscured nearby objects (due to the camera angle) would automatically lower to improve visibility.
